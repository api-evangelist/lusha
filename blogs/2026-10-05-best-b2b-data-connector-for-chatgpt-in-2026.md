Three B2B data vendors have a native app in the ChatGPT Apps directory: Lusha, Apollo and ZoomInfo. All three connect through MCP, so ChatGPT can look up a contact, a company or a signal mid-conversation without a copy-paste step. They differ in what the app can do after the lookup.
